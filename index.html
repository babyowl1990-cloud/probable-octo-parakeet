What a cookie actually is

A cookie is a short piece of text a site asks your browser to keep. The browser then sends it back with every later request to that site. That is the whole mechanism, and it is how a site can tell your second visit from a stranger's first one.

Everything in that list lives in cookies, so the site remembers it without a database or a login.

Try the cookie stash

The TrackLab Cookie Stash extension keeps a copy of this page's cookies, removes them when you leave, and hands them back when you return. To test it:

  1. Scroll to any section and type a note below.
  2. Close this tab. The extension removes the cookies from the browser.
  3. Open http://localhost:8080 again.
  4. You should land in the section you left, the note should be there, and the visit count should be one higher.

With the extension on, that button proves the point: the cookies come straight back on reload. With it off, you become a stranger.
